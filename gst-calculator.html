How to Use the GST Calculator

Calculate tax additions or reverse deductions in seconds:

  1. Select Calculation Type: Choose GST Exclusive (Add GST) if your price is before tax, or GST Inclusive (Remove GST) if your price already includes tax.
  2. Enter Amount: Input the initial transaction value or invoice total.
  3. Choose GST Rate Slab: Click any preset rate (0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) or type a custom tax percentage.
  4. Calculate and Verify: Click Calculate to view Net Base Amount, Total GST Component, Gross Invoice Amount, and the 50/50 CGST & SGST breakdown.

How the GST Formulas Work

1. Adding GST (Exclusive Mode): When the base price excludes tax:

GST Amount = (Base Price × GST Rate) / 100
Total Price = Base Price + GST Amount

2. Removing GST (Inclusive Mode): When the price already contains tax:

Base Price = Total Price / (1 + (GST Rate / 100))
GST Amount = Total Price - Base Price

Intra-State Split: CGST = GST Amount / 2, SGST = GST Amount / 2.

Practical Example of Adding and Removing GST

Adding GST (18% Slab on ₹50,000 base):

  • GST Amount: (₹50,000 × 18) / 100 = ₹9,000
  • CGST (9%): ₹4,500 | SGST (9%): ₹4,500
  • Final billing price: ₹50,000 + ₹9,000 = ₹59,000

Removing GST (Extracting 18% tax from ₹59,000 gross total):

  • Pre-tax base: ₹59,000 / (1 + 0.18) = ₹50,000
  • Extracted GST component: ₹59,000 - ₹50,000 = ₹9,000

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

GST Exclusive means the price displayed is before tax; GST must be added on top. GST Inclusive means the displayed price already incorporates the applicable GST tax.
For intra-state transactions (within the same state), GST is split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For inter-state transactions (between two states), Integrated GST (IGST) is charged in full.
The formula to remove GST is: Base Price = Total Price / (1 + (GST Rate / 100)). For an 18% slab, divide the gross price by 1.18.
