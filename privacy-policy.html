Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

At Finance Calculator Hub (accessible from https://ankuu09.github.io/finance-calculator/), one of our primary priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected and recorded, and how it is used.

1. Zero Collection of Financial Data

Finance Calculator Hub operates as a 100% client-side web utility. All calculations—including loan amounts, salary inputs, investment figures, interest rates, and tax parameters—are evaluated exclusively inside your local device's web browser using JavaScript.

None of your entered numerical values, financial calculations, or simulation results are ever transmitted to or stored on any server. When you close or refresh your browser tab, memory containing calculation inputs is automatically discarded.

2. Use of Local Storage (Browser Preferences)

To enhance user convenience across multiple visits, we utilize your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly for:

  • Saving your chosen currency preference (such as ₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP, or ¥ JPY) so that subsequent pages display your preferred currency symbol.

This data remains solely on your local device and is never sent across the internet. You can clear this preference at any time by clearing your browser's site cookies and local storage.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like many static websites hosted on modern platforms (such as GitHub Pages), standard anonymous web server logs may temporarily capture non-personally identifiable technical information, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and operating system version
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Date and time stamps

These logs are used solely to monitor platform health, prevent malicious traffic or DDoS attacks, and understand general technical compatibility. They are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense Compliance)

To maintain free access to our financial tools, Finance Calculator Hub may host third-party advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other advertising networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Children's Information

Finance Calculator Hub does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information.

6. Consent & Policy Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. Any future updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
