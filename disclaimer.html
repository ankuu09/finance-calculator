Financial Disclaimer

Important Notice Regarding Estimates & Professional Advice
Summary Notice: All calculation tools, formulas, outputs, charts, and schedules on Finance Calculator Hub are provided for informational and educational simulation purposes only. They do not constitute certified financial, tax, investment, or legal advice.

1. Nature of Calculations (Estimates Only)

The financial calculators provided on this platform use standard mathematical algorithms (such as monthly reducing-balance formulas for loan EMIs, annuity-due compounding for SIPs, and periodic compound growth for fixed deposits).

While these formulas represent industry standards, the figures generated are illustrative estimates. Real-world financial results will vary due to factors beyond basic mathematical models, including:

  • Bank Rounding Practices: Commercial institutions may use daily interest compounding, exact-day fractional year calculations (365/366 days), or proprietary monthly rounding rules.
  • Processing & Administrative Fees: Loan origination fees, stamp duties, file charges, and mandatory insurance premiums are not included in baseline principal and interest numbers.
  • Floating & Variable Interest Rates: If you hold a floating-rate home loan or credit line, changes in central bank benchmark rates will adjust your ongoing EMI or tenure over time.
  • Market Volatility in Investments: Mutual funds, equities, and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) are subject to systemic market risk. Assumed growth rates (e.g., 12% p.a. in SIP) are hypothetical assumptions and are not guaranteed.
  • Tax Implications: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), capital gains tax (LTCG / STCG), and cess surcharges depend on individual tax residency and current statutory laws.

2. No Financial Advice or Client Relationship

Using this website or interacting with any tool does not create an advisor-client, fiduciary, or professional relationship between you and Finance Calculator Hub. We do not evaluate your personal risk tolerance, financial obligations, credit score, or individual tax bracket.

3. Always Consult Licensed Professionals

Before entering into any binding credit contract, borrowing funds, prepaying mortgages, or committing capital to investment vehicles, you should always consult with a certified financial planner (CFP), chartered accountant (CA/CPA), or accredited bank loan officer.
