How the Loan EMI Formula Works

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) represents a fixed sum payable to the lender each month. The payment is derived using the standard reducing-balance amortization equation:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)n] / [(1 + r)n - 1]
  • P: Principal loan amount borrowed
  • r: Monthly interest rate (Annual Interest Rate / 12 / 100)
  • n: Total number of monthly installments (Tenure Years × 12)

Practical Example of Loan EMI

Assume you borrow a home loan of ₹25,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 8.5% for a tenure of 20 years:

  • Total repayment tenure: 20 years × 12 = 240 monthly payments
  • Monthly interest rate (r): 8.5% / 12 = 0.7083% per month (0.007083)
  • Calculated monthly EMI: ₹21,696
  • Total repayment amount over 20 years: 240 × ₹21,696 = ₹52,06,948
  • Total cumulative interest charges: ₹52,06,948 - ₹25,00,000 = ₹27,06,948
Important Financial Estimation Notice All EMI projections are mathematical estimates based on constant reducing-balance interest rates. Floating rate loans may change over the term. Processing fees, statutory stamp duty, and administrative charges are not included. Please check loan schedules with your lending institution.
