How the SIP Formula Works

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) works on the principle of compound interest applied to recurring regular installments (an annuity due). Because each installment has a different compounding duration, the future value formula sums the compounding growth of every individual monthly deposit:

FV = P × [((1 + i)n - 1) / i] × (1 + i)
  • FV: Future value (projected maturity value)
  • P: Amount invested each month
  • i: Periodic monthly rate of return (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • n: Total number of monthly installments (Years × 12)

Practical Example of SIP Growth

Suppose you invest ₹10,000 every month for 10 years with an expected annual return of 12%:

  • Total installments: 10 years × 12 months = 120 deposits
  • Total capital invested: 120 × ₹10,000 = ₹12,00,000
  • Monthly interest rate (i): 12% / 12 = 1% (0.01 per month)
  • Projected maturity balance (FV): approximately ₹23,23,391
  • Estimated compounding returns: ₹23,23,391 - ₹12,00,000 = ₹11,23,391

Notice that in 10 years, compounding returns represent nearly half of the entire portfolio value.
